# SafeCircle - GBV Emergency Support App

**Business Analysis & Solution Design Project**

A comprehensive solution design for a gender-based violence emergency response application, addressing South Africa's high GBV statistics through accessible, real-time support.

---

## Project Overview

**Academic Project** - 2nd Year BSc Information Technology  
**Institution:** North-West University (NWU)  
**Team:** SafeTech Solutions  
**My Role:** Project Lead & Systems Designer

---

## Problem Statement

South Africa has the highest GBV statistics globally according to the World Health Organization:
- 1 in 3 women experience physical or sexual violence in their lifetime
- Emergency response times average 27+ minutes
- Many victims remain silent due to fear and lack of accessible support

**Root Causes:**
- Unequal power dynamics between genders
- Substance abuse
- Negative gender stereotypes
- Lack of social and emotional support systems

---

## Proposed Solution

**SafeCircle** is a mobile application designed to provide immediate, accessible support to GBV victims through:

### Core System Components

**1. User Profiling & Registration**
- Anonymous or verified user registration
- Personalized safety profiles
- Privacy-first approach

**2. SOS Emergency System**
- Single-touch emergency alarm
- Automatic location sharing to emergency contacts
- Silent audio recording
- Offline functionality

**3. Anonymous Reporting**
- Guided reporting process
- Evidence submission capability
- Secure reporting to authorities

**4. Support Network Access**
- Connection to national and local support agencies
- Educational resources
- 24/7 expert support network

---

## Key Features

**Emergency Resources**
- Panic button with real-time location tracking

- Quick access to emergency services

**Privacy & Anonymity**
- Anonymous reporting options

- Privacy-protected evidence submission

**Community Support**

**Education & Prevention**
- GBV awareness content
- Early warning sign recognition
- Healthy relationship education

## Business Impact Analysis

### Measurable Outcomes

**Response Time Reduction**
- Current average: 27+ minutes
- Target with SafeCircle: Under 8 minutes
- 70% reduction in emergency response time

**Community Impact**
- Breaks cycle of silence around GBV
- Connects fragmented support services
- Promotes prevention through education
- Builds long-term community resilience
